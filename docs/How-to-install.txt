DeskMemo (桌面备忘) — How to install
=====================================

1. Drag "桌面备忘" into the "Applications" folder next to it.
2. Open Applications and double-click "桌面备忘".

On first launch macOS may say it "cannot verify the developer".
This app is not notarized by Apple (that costs money) — it is not malware. To open it:

  1. Click "Done" in the dialog (NOT "Move to Trash").
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to "Security".
  3. Next to "桌面备忘 was blocked", click "Open Anyway" and enter your password.
  4. Double-click "桌面备忘" again. You will not be asked again.

When asked "Allow access to Reminders?", click Allow to show your reminders on the desktop.

Requires macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later. Works on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

-------------------------------------

安装方法

1. 把「桌面备忘」拖到旁边的「应用程序」文件夹里。
2. 打开「应用程序」，双击「桌面备忘」。

第一次打开如果提示「无法验证开发者」：点「完成」（不要点「移到废纸篓」），
然后打开 系统设置 → 隐私与安全性，往下找到「已阻止使用"桌面备忘"」，点「仍要打开」，输入开机密码。只需要一次。

打开后询问是否允许访问「提醒事项」，点「允许」即可。
